# Material Sources
## Fruits
All fruit prefabs used in the FruitPrefab directory, as well as the .fbx models were taken from the 
[Kawaii Land Asset Pack](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/kawaii-land-218205). That includes prefabs, models, materials
and textures/shaders. The sliced fruit models were made manually by us in Blender.

## Scene
All scene elements are taken from the [Kawaii Land Asset Pack](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/kawaii-land-218205).
The demo that is supplied with the Asset Pack was used as a base for designing the game scene. Changed aspects about the scene to supply
the gameplay mechanics were also achieved with assets from this pack. This includes prefabs, models, materials and textures/shaders.

## Swords
The sword prefabs and models were taken from the [Hand Pained Swords Pack](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/weapons/hand-painted-swords-pack-15543).
Their models, prefabs and materials are in the swords directory in the External Materials.

## Button
The button .fbx was supplied by a friend of ours who is proficient with Blender. The prefab was made by us, 
and the material is a standard material from Unity with a few tweaks.
